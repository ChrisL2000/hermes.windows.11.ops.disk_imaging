## Pitfalls actually hit in this session (all now encoded in SKILL.md)
- User-typed IP was wrong (192 vs 198) — trust `net use`.
- L: letter stale — use UNC.
- A "writable" probe printed success on a non-existent drive (swallowed exception) —
  must read back.
- `Get-Content -AsByteStream` (PS7) failed on PS 5.1 — use .NET.
- `1073741824` arrived corrupted in generated script text — use `[int64](1GB)`.
- Int32 overflow in the benchmark loop at >2 GiB remaining — Int64-first Min.
- Inline PowerShell in .cmd: PS single-quoted strings don't expand `$t`; cmd needs two
  literal backslashes for UNC; testing from bash mangles `$t`.
